# Ideal follower

## Summary

**Inferred from content, not audience analytics:** the primary follower is a SaaS founder who wants demand, profitable growth and control of their company. A growing 2026 segment is the GTM operator, agency or non-engineer learning practical AI workflows. These are editorial audience hypotheses, not verified follower demographics.

## Language and objections

Adam's language repeatedly addresses feeling stuck, the pain of a negative dashboard, the urge to hire or raise money, and the desire for freedom. These are **Adam's descriptions of the reader**, not direct customer-interview quotes. His July 31 post responds to founders seeing churn and slowing growth by exposing his own unresolved hypotheses. His September advice challenges the belief that funding repairs weak product-market fit.

Likely objections to preserve as hypotheses: a tiny team may hide shared labor; audience-led acquisition may not transfer to a founder with no audience; his product or market may not resemble the reader's; staying lean does not eliminate churn. The August MoltSets plan itself discloses a shared team and distribution assumptions. [Source](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-08-25-7498049303853191168.md).

## Historical campaign observations

In the August 29, 2025 Understory walkthrough, the agency ranks RB2B's LinkedIn campaign personas as founders, agencies, marketers, then salespeople, using **total signup volume and CTR**. It hypothesizes that founders and agencies could implement the product more quickly. Adam is explicitly absent from this episode: this is an agency report about historical campaigns, not Adam's own audience analysis. No budget normalization, paid conversion, audience proportions or causal test is supplied. [Source: 02:21–02:27 and 10:51–13:06](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/EdDAHrvBDPM/EdDAHrvBDPM.md).

The useful editorial hypothesis is to consider both interest and the reader's ability to act: a salesperson may value a visitor signal while someone else controls installation. Do not turn the agency's hypothesis into a fixed judgment about a profession. Its Meta reporting was then by creative, with persona attribution only proposed, so the LinkedIn ranking cannot be generalized across channels or to the newer MoltSets offer. [Source: 22:49–23:53](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/EdDAHrvBDPM/EdDAHrvBDPM.md).

## Follower versus product buyer

Adam explicitly qualifies building in public by the overlap between the founder's experience and the intended buyer's interests. In the Base44 conversation, he recommends choosing the platform around that fit and values the growing archive and improving craft; beginners should not expect his established reach. Maor separately questions whether the same founder audience would fit a product for lawyers or accountants. This supports testing audience fit, not assuming that founder storytelling suits every product. [July 20, 2025 publication, 45:01–47:16](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/1f19uvtzNds/1f19uvtzNds.md).

In the positioning conversation, Anthony Pierri challenges Adam's assumption that buyers already understand person-level visitor identification: it may describe the LinkedIn audience he currently reaches. Adam accepts that correction and contrasts those readers with people outside that audience. The useful boundary is that the current distribution channel can shape apparent buyer awareness; it does not establish the wider market's knowledge or willingness to pay. His broader product described in the same passage was still unbuilt, and the newer scope reversal in [[strategy/learnings]] takes precedence. [July 20, 2025 publication, 32:42–34:37](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/M9Pw3qJdNYE/M9Pw3qJdNYE.md).

A reader can value entrepreneurship stories without having a use for visitor identification or contact data. The August MoltSets plan describes RB2B's then-current limits as US visitor traffic and enough traffic to justify a paid plan; it distinguishes API consumers and flat-file data buyers for MoltSets. These are dated product-strategy statements, not a current product eligibility check or a reason to treat every engager as a lead.

For content selection, start with the reader's problem and one pillar. For product-oriented content, establish the relevant use case and an available next step separately. See [[strategy/funnel]].
