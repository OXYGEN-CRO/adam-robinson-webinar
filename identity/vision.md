# Vision

## Summary

**Proposed synthesis:** a founder can build valuable, profitable software and data businesses with a small, capable team, while retaining the freedom and energy to choose a life worth living. Adam describes company building as his creative expression, rather than a task he would automatically stop after becoming financially secure. [June 25, 2026](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-06-25-7475952078696259585.md).

## What the future looks like in his current thesis

His August 25 MoltSets plan bets that agent/API consumers will become a much larger share of the contact-data market over the following three to five years. He proposes a low-priced, API-only offer supported by existing profitable data businesses, founder distribution and automated support. His **$24m ARR in 24 months with fewer than five full-time employees is a forecast**, not achieved proof or a complete staffing-cost picture. The plan explicitly names shared data, design and sales contributors. [Source](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-08-25-7498049303853191168.md).

## What the companies make possible

The present strategy need not force every existing product to grow indefinitely. In July he says he wants RB2B to remain a simple visitor-identity business around a $9–11m ARR range, instead of joining an expanding signal-feature contest. He presents MoltSets as the next adjacent opportunity. The range is his desired business shape, not a guarantee. [July 31](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-07-31-7488990110525353984.md).

Across these statements, cash generation funds additional experiments and supports an enduring team and a calm schedule. The goal is not literal absence of humans; the shared organization and experienced co-founders remain essential. See [[identity/values]], [[identity/life-goals]] and [[identity/proof]].
