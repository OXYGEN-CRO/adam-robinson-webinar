## Discovery method he describes

Review leading creators' recent successful posts, collect the questions people ask in the niche, and separate hooks, structures and endings in a swipe file. He recommends bringing one's own story to the structure and eventually moving beyond templates. This is Adam's stated method, not verification of the performance of third-party tools he mentions. [Source: April 16, 02:43–03:42 and 05:43–05:59](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/eUzqVJpZPP4/eUzqVJpZPP4.md).
