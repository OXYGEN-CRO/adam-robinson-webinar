The user rejected the 20-second v02 as poor motion design and requested research into much higher-quality launch films and skills. Shorter runtime is explicitly preferred if it allows better craft. ## Review boundary

The v02 design was rejected by the user. Its technical checks do not establish visual quality. The central workflow and sample people are illustrated, not a recording of the live product. “No GTM engineer required” is user-supplied campaign direction, not the result of a nontechnical-user test. The unlimited message retains visible core-data and plan-limit scope; phone allowances remain separate. Claude usage is not represented as included in MoltSets pricing.

Before turning the illustration into a public product-proof segment, replace it with an actual capture or decide to keep it explicitly illustrative. Current product pages describe people search and the Claude Code integration. Previously identified billing-doc ambiguity remains recorded in the claim-source file; this cut does not assert “no credits,” a specific price or that signup is already public.

The imported audio provenance remains in [[brand/audio]]. Technical decode and loudness checks do not establish subjective listening or commercial-use entitlement. No publication, outreach, live data collection, paid generation or remote upload is part of the design delivery.
